# Security policy

We take the security of Otapp's platforms and our customers' data seriously, and we
welcome reports from security researchers.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **[development@otapp.net](mailto:development@otapp.net)** with the subject line
`SECURITY: <short summary>`. Please include:

- the affected product, URL, app version or repository;
- what the issue is and its likely impact;
- steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept when it is safe to share;
- how we can reach you for follow-up.

Please do **not** open a public GitHub issue for a security problem.

## What to expect

- We aim to acknowledge your report within **3 business days**.
- We will keep you updated while we investigate and fix the issue.
- We will agree a disclosure timeline with you, and credit you if you wish.

## Scope

In scope: Otapp's production websites, apps and APIs, and the repositories in this
organisation.

Out of scope:

- denial-of-service or load testing;
- social engineering, phishing or physical attacks;
- spam, or findings that need a compromised device or account;
- reports from automated scanners with no demonstrated impact.

## Safe harbour

We will not pursue legal action against research carried out in good faith under this
policy. That means you avoid privacy violations, data destruction and service
disruption, only access data needed to demonstrate the issue, and give us reasonable
time to fix it before any disclosure.

This file is the default security policy for Otapp repositories that do not define
their own `SECURITY.md`.
